Standard // What Counts as Proof

A case study should make the evidence easier to inspect.

Raptor will use case studies to show the problem, the work performed, the result, and the evidence type. If a number was never measured, it will not be invented later for a prettier marketing page.

01State the operating context.

What kind of website, system, constraint, or business problem was involved?

02Separate action from outcome.

What was actually changed, built, aligned, migrated, or verified?

03Label the evidence source.

System delivery, direct observation, analytics evidence, or client-reported result.

04Do not manufacture causality.

If the evidence supports correlation or a reported improvement, say exactly that.

Raptor // Evidence Discipline

The same rule applies to marketing and diagnosis.

Detection is not diagnosis. Activity is not outcome. A before-and-after screenshot is not automatically causality.

Raptor's public proof should be inspectable enough that a prospective client can tell what was done, what changed, and where the evidence stops.
